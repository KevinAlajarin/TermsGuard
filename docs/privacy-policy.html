Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Short version: TermsGuard AI has no servers. Your API key and the page content you analyze never pass through us — they go directly from your browser to the AI provider you choose. We collect nothing.

1. What data is stored on your device

TermsGuard AI stores the following data locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us:

  • Your AI provider API key (Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic)
  • Your preferences: language, analysis mode, and auto-detection setting
  • Your analysis history (up to 5 recent results)

2. What data is sent to third parties

When you click "Analyze", the text content of the legal page you are viewing is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you have configured (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic). This request is made using your own API key.

  • We have no visibility into this request — it goes directly from your browser to the provider
  • The data transmitted is governed by the privacy policy of the provider you chose
  • No analysis is triggered automatically — only when you explicitly press "Analyze"

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not have any servers or backend infrastructure
  • We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • We do not identify users or track behavior across sites
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize any data

4. Permissions used and why

  • storage — to save your API key, preferences, and history locally on your device
  • activeTab — to read the URL and title of the tab you are on
  • scripting — to extract the text content of the legal page when you request an analysis
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — required to detect legal pages on any website and to run the text extraction script on the active tab

5. Children's privacy

TermsGuard AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
